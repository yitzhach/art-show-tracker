ARTIST INFORMATION

The 22nd Annual Maple Hollow Fine Arts Festival

Saturday & Sunday, April 17 & 18, 2027
10am – 5pm both days
Riverside Park, Maple Hollow, North Carolina

Maple Hollow's juried outdoor fine art festival brings 140 artists to Riverside Park each spring. Attendance last year was about 30,000.

How to apply
Applications open October 1, 2026 and close January 10, 2027.
Jury fee: $35 (non-refundable)
Booth fee: $375 for a 10x10 space
Jury results will be emailed by February 15, 2027.

Questions? Email artists@example.org
Apply online: https://example.org/maple-hollow/apply
